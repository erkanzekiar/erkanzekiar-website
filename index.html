05 // PROFILE & ROLES

Biography & Academic Depth

Bridging sociological analysis with intuitive musical craftsmanship.

Artist Statement

Erkan Zeki Ar is an Istanbul-born multi-instrumentalist, composer, and music producer with over 10 years of professional immersion in contemporary music. Having graduated in Sociology from Marmara University before completing his Master's in Music and Performing Arts at Yıldız Technical University, his artistic outlook is distinctly shaped by a double consciousness: analytical sociology and instinctive sound sculpting.

His master’s thesis, "Alienation in Music Production", examines modern studio workflows and the psychology of sonic creation. This research directly informs his production style: organic, emotionally attentive, and rich in textural space.

As a Musical Director and session guitarist, he has led ensembles at premier venues including Zorlu PSM, Nardis Jazz Club, Babylon, and landmark festivals including Akbank Jazz Festival and IKSV Istanbul Jazz Festival. Currently expanding his base to Germany, Erkan works across acoustic, fretless, and electric instruments, producing records and touring internationally.

Musical Director (2016–Present)

Ensemble curation, live arrangement, repertoire direction (Selin Sümbültepe and touring collectives).

Audio Engineer & Producer (2018–Present)

Tracking, mixing, mastering, original theatre (Apsolit) and film (Bağlanma Hali) scores.

Educator (2024–Present)

Ensemble instructor at Randy Esen’s S.O.S. (Singers Only Space), focusing on orchestra leadership and chart writing.
